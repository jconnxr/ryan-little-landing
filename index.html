Meet Ryan. He'd rather know you than sell you.

Ryan has spent more than five years helping Oklahomans through Medicare. He's a dad and a true people person, and he got into this work because he likes making people's lives a little easier.

He doesn't believe in dropping people into random plans. He takes the time to learn who his clients are, which doctors they see and what matters to them, then looks for coverage that keeps their doctors and keeps life running smoothly. That's why he works independently: so he's working for the person in front of him, not for one company.

His goal is simple: take a complicated process and make it feel easy.
